The Book Not in vain
En Ru

Enjoy

If you’ve been waiting for straight advice, here it is. The things I wish someone had explained to me long ago.

Don’t lie. You’re only cheating yourself. Anxious thoughts lie to you. Fear distorts reality badly. Don’t believe it, and move on.

Integrity comes from intent. Think, say, and do what’s right — not what’s easy, not what’s popular. Show your true colors. Sooner or later it works out.

Act, don’t react. Do everything slowly. Faster is not better. We type faster than we write — and soon we’ll type whole pages without touching a key.

Body is a friend. Get a blood test, understand the results, do the profiling. Know thyself. Train your head, your mind, and your brain too. Fit body, fit brain. Meditation is fasting for the mind.

Perfect day every day. The perfect day is the only day. If I don’t go to the gym, that means something isn’t right.

Always do your best. It’s easy to do — if the intent is true.

What would you do if you weren’t afraid? The answer is your real life, and the door to it opens from the inside.

Not my final form

I’m a work in progress. This is not my final form.

Think forward.
