Freedom

Live the life you want to live

Freedom is the chance to live your life the way you want. By that definition alone, nothing matters more. And it’s within reach even if everyone around you stays exactly as they are. So the real question is this: if freedom means living the way you want, how free are you right now?

Missing a train only hurts if you run after it. Same with falling short of someone else’s success.

Traps

Watch out for the traps, the comforts and obligations that quietly eat up your life. Harry Browne described them fifty years ago, and everything he said still holds. No politics, no manifestos, no nonsense, just a clear view of what holds you back and a few working ways out: accept uncertainty, and keep the power over yourself.

The first step to controlling your thoughts is to clear your mind of fear and everything that grows out of it: doubt, disbelief, greed, envy, jealousy and superstition.

Freedom means working up the courage to take your prison apart brick by brick.

But those prison walls exist only because you have chosen to allow them to stand.
Harry Browne

Voluntary surrender leads to loss of freedom.

Freedoms

You can opt out. You can step out of the automatic reaction at any moment. There’s nowhere to quit to and nowhere to quit from. Just don’t play the games whose rules you don’t like.

Become your own highest authority. That’s sovereignty: inner independence, where the final word in your life is yours. From there, you no longer need the world’s permission.

In 1997, a book called The Sovereign Individual came out. It predicted Bitcoin twelve years before it appeared: digital money no state can control. The world is now unfolding exactly the way it said.

To be free, it’s enough to stop handing your one life over to fear. There isn’t that much of it.
