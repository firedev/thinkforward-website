Children

You, your partner and your children: get those three right, and you have a family.

Family is a bike with training wheels. It’s where a person learns to balance before riding off alone.

And parenting takes less than you think. I’m for minimum parenting. Don’t wear yourself out trying to engineer the perfect child: they’ll grow up like you anyway, so the work to do is on yourself.

Don’t raise your kids — they’ll turn out like you regardless. Work on yourself. Raise yourself. — Stoyanov

Berne wrote a whole book about it: children living out their parents’ script. And you hand that script down by example; lectures have nothing to do with it.

My kids are their own people, not literally an extension of me. Well, yes, they carry me on, but they’re something new, something else. In Russian, children are called the flowers of life. To me they’re mushrooms: mushrooms grow out of the mycelium, and we’re the ground they grow in. Teach them early what we learn late.

James Breakwell sets three goals: your kids can support themselves, they grow up decent people, and they don’t blame you for everything that’s wrong in their lives.

Don’t let them make you stop liking them. Relax. You and the kids will be just fine.

Kids need feeding. Let’s talk money.
